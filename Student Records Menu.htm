Arts, B.A.&Sc., Kinesiology, Management, and Science students only: Change your program, major, minor, honours, etc.

  • Changes made in December–February are effective for the Winter Term onwards.
  • To change your curriculum for the Summer Term, contact your Student Affairs Office or Service Point.

  Deferred Exam Application
Apply to defer an exam or check the status of an application. Special note for Summer courses: the Minerva application for deferred exams for summer courses is only available for courses offered within the Faculties of Arts and Science. If your course is offered by a different Faculty, please submit your application directly to that Faculty’s Student Affairs Office.
  Supplemental Exam Application
Apply to take a supplemental exam or check the status of your application.

  Degree Evaluation
Compare your academic record to the requirements of a specific program (only for students admitted in Winter 2019 and earlier).
THIS IS NOT AN OFFICIAL AUDIT AND MAY CONTAIN ERRORS (Degree audits are automatically generated. Students are encouraged to speak to their advisor to compare their academic record against specific program requirements). In some cases, courses with the S/U grading option are misinterpreted by the Degree Evaluation tool. Students that have applied the S/U grading option to non-elective courses should contact their program advisors to confirm their program requirements.
